Hades Exynos8895 EAS + frequency-only OC
================================================

Branch: android10-eas-oc
Base: Hades Android 10 (Linux 4.4.302)

CPU ceilings exposed from existing firmware ECT levels:
  LITTLE / Cortex-A53: 2002000 kHz (stock 1690000)
  big / Exynos M2:     2652000 kHz (stock 2314000)

GPU:
  Requested safety ceiling: 746000 kHz
  Firmware ECT levels: 839000, 764000, 683000, 572000, 546000, ...
  Selected maximum: 683000 kHz

The Mali driver requires an exact ECT/ACPM frequency match. 746000 is not
an actual firmware OPP, while 764000 exceeds the requested safety ceiling.
The 839000 and 764000 rows remain in the source table but are unreachable
because both CAL and the Mali device-tree maximum are capped at 683000.

This port intentionally does not expose voltage tables, voltage offsets,
or regulator-step controls. Firmware continues to own voltage selection.
CPU boot PM QoS is held at the stock 1690000/2314000 kHz ceilings; higher
frequencies become available after the boot lock expires and can be chosen
by schedutil or a kernel manager through the normal cpufreq interfaces.
